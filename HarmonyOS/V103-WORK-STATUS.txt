and trackers. System notification permission remains distinct, requested only

notifications remain independent.

network identified by NetworkKit and binds BT to its interface; unknown/VPN bearers
wait conservatively. Never enumerate or bypass alternate networks. Native maintenance
checks every second; disallowed mode pauses the session and closes BT listeners,
TCP/uTP/discovery/NAT services, without altering manual pause or queue preferences.

HTTP and downloaded-file playback remain independent. This is an app transfer policy,
not an OS firewall guarantee; real phone Wi-Fi/cellular transitions need acceptance.
